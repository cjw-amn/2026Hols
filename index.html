Thursday
Beach Day around Bude
Relax▶

Settle in and explore. Summerleaze Beach has the famous sea pool — calm and shallow, perfect for little ones.

  • 🏰 Sandcastles & rockpooling at low tide
  • 🍦 Ice cream on the front
  • 🌳 Crooklets for more space, or the canal path for a buggy-friendly stroll
  • 🛶 Bude Canal — hire a rowing boat or pedalo on the calm water (gentle & great for the kids)
  • 🚇 The Bude Tunnel — the legendary 70m covered walkway by Sainsbury's that went viral as a "tourist attraction"! A daft, free 2-minute giggle — strike a pose 📸
Tide tip: Check tide times — rockpools are best a couple of hours either side of low tide. And yes, the Bude Tunnel really is just a tunnel — that's the joke. 😄

Friday
Padstow & RIB Sea Safari
Harbour day▶

Pretty harbour town. Crabbing off the quayside is a huge hit with little ones! 🦀

⛵ Booked: Wavehunters RIB Sea Safari
  • 🚤 1-hour RIB sea safari with Wavehunters UK Ltd — Friday 17th July, 2:45pm
  • 📍 Meet at the Wavehunters shop, Padstow — aim to arrive ~15–20 min early to check in & kit up
  • 🦭 Eyes peeled for seals, dolphins & seabirds!
🐟 Around the harbour
  • 🍟 Fish & chips by the water
  • 🚴 The Camel Trail is flat & great if you fancy a buggy/bike stroll
  • ⛴️ Black Tor Ferry — tiny hop across to Rock beach & back; a fun mini boat ride for pennies
Tips: RIBs are bouncy & splashy — warm layers under the waterproofs, and do lunch before the 2:45pm trip. Grab a crabbing line & bucket from a harbour shop — bacon = best bait!
🧭 Directions to the Wavehunters shop, Padstow 🧭 Directions to Padstow (Link Road car park, PL28 8AX) 🌊 Padstow tide times

Sunday
Eden Project
Big day out▶

~50 min drive. Giant biomes, jungle, play areas and lots to spot. A full day — go early!

  • 🌴 Rainforest Biome (warm — dress in layers you can peel off)
  • 🛝 Outdoor play & the "invisible worlds" zone
  • 🎟️ Book tickets online in advance — they're cheaper & valid all year
Tip: The 3 yr old may flag by mid-afternoon — bring the buggy/carrier. There's a land train if legs get tired.
🧭 Directions to Eden Project (PL24 2SG)
🍦
Monday
Chill Day
Slow morning▶

Another easy one after Eden. Favourite beach again, mini golf, or a lazy harbour wander.

Tip: A rest day here keeps little legs fresh for Tintagel's steep steps tomorrow.
🏰
Tuesday
Tintagel Castle
Legends!▶

~35 min drive. Clifftop castle ruins, the dramatic bridge, and King Arthur legends. ⚔️

  • 🌉 The footbridge is amazing but there are lots of steep steps
  • 🧚 Merlin's Cave on the beach at low tide (check tides!)
  • 🐉 Spot the Gallos bronze statue & the carved Merlin face
Tip: A back carrier beats a buggy here — the terrain is steep & uneven. Hold little hands near edges. It's the last full Bude day, so start a bit of pre-packing tonight to keep the move to Somerset calm.
🧭 Directions to Tintagel (visitor centre car park, PL34 0AA) 🌊 Tintagel tide times

Thursday
Cheddar Gorge
Explore▶

Britain's biggest gorge — right on the doorstep! Towering cliffs and caves. 🦇

  • 🕳️ Gough's Cave — stroller-friendly-ish, magical for kids
  • 🧀 Cheese & fudge tasting in the village
  • 🐐 Spot the wild goats on the cliffs
Tip: The clifftop walk & Jacob's Ladder (274 steps!) are tough for tiny legs — the caves & village are the easy win with this age group.
🏡
Friday
Day Trip → then Home
Last day▶

One more adventure before the drive home (~3 hrs to LU7 3JN):

  • 🐉 Wookey Hole — caves, fairy garden, dinosaurs & play (kid heaven, nearby)
  • ⛪ Wells — England's smallest city, swans & cathedral
  • 🦆 Glastonbury or a farm park if the weather's kind
Tip: Set off home after an early lunch & aim to drive through nap time — then a comfort stop halfway. 🚗💤

🎮 Toddler-friendly games
I Spy (colours) — for the 3 yr old, use colours not letters: "I spy something… green!" 🟢
Spot it! — first to see a tractor / red car / cow / bridge wins a point 🚜
Animal sounds — name an animal, everyone makes the noise 🐮
Sticker books & magnetic toys — mess-free travel activities 📒
Audiobooks & sing-alongs — Julia Donaldson stories are car gold 🎵
Snack countdown — "a treat at the next services!" works wonders 🍪
Guess the animal — give 3 clues: "I'm big, I'm grey, I have a long trunk…" 🐘
Copy the clap — clap a little rhythm, the kids echo it back. Get sillier each round 👏
Colour hunt — pick a colour, first to spot 5 things that colour out the window 🔴
Loud lion, quiet mouse — sing a verse ROARING loud, then whisper-quiet 🦁🐭
Magic word — pick a silly word ("sausages!") and everyone shouts it when you pass a caravan 🌭
Teddy's questions — a teddy "whispers" silly questions to the parent to ask the kids: "have you ever seen a purple cow?" 🧸

🚗 Driving

  • Total trip driving ~10–11 hrs over the week
  • Plan a stop every ~2 hrs with the kids
  • Keep a full water bottle & sick bag in the car 🤢
  • Check fuel before remote Cornish roads
  • 🅿️ Cornwall car parks that take the JustPark wallet (PDF) — pay by app, no scrabbling for coins

🌦️ Weather plan

  • Cornwall is changeable — pack for 4 seasons
  • Rainy day picks: Eden, caves, soft play, aquariums
  • Sunny: beaches, gorge, harbours
  • Suncream + hats and waterproofs!

🦀 Beach safety

  • Swim only at lifeguarded beaches, between the flags 🏴
  • Check tide times daily (cut-off risk in coves)
  • Bude Sea Pool is the calmest spot for little ones
  • Wristbands with your phone number for the kids

🎟️ Book ahead

  • Eden Project tickets (cheaper online)
  • Tintagel Castle (English Heritage)
  • Any Padstow boat trip
  • Cheddar Gorge & Caves / Wookey Hole
