🗺️ The Plan 2 nights

Tap any day to open it up. A packed little weekend — trains, moors, castles and a proper Sunday lunch! 🍗

🚂
Friday 21 August
Drive up → National Railway Museum → Caravan
Travel + trains▶

~7:30am — Leave home (LU7 3JN) with Grandma aboard. ~3¼ hrs to York plus a leg-stretch stop, so aim to arrive late morning. ☕

🚂 National Railway Museum (free!)
  • 🕙 Open 10am–5pm — entry is free, but book free tickets online in advance to skip the queue
  • 🅿️ Museum car park is pay & display — sat nav YO26 4XJ
  • 🚂 Giant locos in the Great Hall — Mallard is the fastest steam train EVER (a big hit with a 5 yr old!)
  • 🔬 Wonderlab — brilliant hands-on science gallery for kids (small extra charge, worth a look)
  • 🍴 Lunch at the museum café or picnic — there's plenty of space
👵 Drop Grandma at Aunty Anne's

Mid-afternoon: ~20 min drive to 5 The Avenue, Park Estate, Haxby, YO32 3EH. Quick cuppa & a wee stop for the kids!

🏕️ On to the caravan

Then ~35 min from Haxby to Brickyard Lakes Country Park, Amotherby Lane, Amotherby, Malton YO17 6UP. Check in, unpack, and explore the two lakes before tea. 🦆

Tip: Pack the "car bag" within reach — snacks, water, wipes, comfort toys and a few surprise activities. The museum has buggy-friendly lifts everywhere.

🐑
Saturday 22 August
Hole of Horcum & Goathland (with Aunty Anne & Grandma)
Moors day▶

Morning — Aunty Anne & Grandma drive up to the country park (~35 min from Haxby). Show them the caravan, coffee by the lakes, then off to the moors! 🚗

🕳️ Hole of Horcum (~35 min via Pickering)
  • 🅿️ Park at Saltergate car park (pay & display) — the massive viewpoint is right next to the car park, so it's perfect for Grandma and little legs alike
  • 🧙 Tell the kids the legend: Wade the Giant scooped out the whole valley with his hand to throw mud at his wife! Can they see his finger marks?
  • 🚶 A short flat stroll along the rim is plenty — no need to hike down into the bowl
🐑 Goathland (~15 min further on)
  • 🐑 Sheep wander free all over the village — the kids will love spotting them on the green (look, don't chase — and watch for them on the road!)
  • 🚗 Aidensfield Garage — vintage cars & the old funeral van from Heartbeat, fun to peek at
  • 🍦 Ice creams & gift shops around the green, tearoom or pub lunch (the Goathland Hotel is the "Aidensfield Arms")
  • 🧙‍♂️ Fun fact: Goathland station is Hogsmeade in the first Harry Potter film! We're not riding the steam railway — but a free 2-minute peek from the road bridge to wave if a train puffs in is optional magic 🚂
  • ⛔ Skip Mallyan Spout waterfall this time — the path is steep & slippery, a bit much for a 3 yr old
🍟 Fish & chip tea back at the park
  • Head back for ~4:30–5pm and do a chippy run into Malton (~8 min from the park):
  • 🥇 Peasey Hill Fish & Chips, Malton — top-rated locally, open Sat teatime ~4:30–8pm
  • 🥈 Stuarts of Driffield, 56 Castlegate, Malton — open Sat 4–8pm, ☎ 01653 694037
  • 🍴 Eat at the caravan by the lakes, then Aunty Anne (& Grandma) head home to Haxby 👋
Tip: The moors are always cooler & windier than the vale — pack an extra layer each even in August. Coins/card for the two car parks. Bring the carrier for the 3 yr old at Hole of Horcum.

🏰
Sunday 23 August
Clifford's Tower → Sunday Lunch → Outlet → Home
Last day▶

~9:00am — Pack the car & check out of the caravan, then ~35 min into York.

🏰 Clifford's Tower — for opening at 10am
  • 🕙 Opens 10am (English Heritage) — book tickets online; arriving at opening beats the crowds
  • 🅿️ Castle car park is right next door on Tower Street
  • 🪜 55 steps up the grassy mound — a fun charge for the 5 yr old, carrier for the 3 yr old; buggies stay at the bottom
  • 👀 Roof deck with the best views in York — spot the Minster! Takes ~45 min–1 hr, perfect timing
🍗 12:30pm — Sunday lunch at Aunty Anne's

~20 min drive up to Haxby (YO32 3EH). Yorkshire puddings in Yorkshire — as it should be! Don't forget something for the table 🍷🌼

🛍️ If there's time: York Designer Outlet
  • 📍 St Nicholas Avenue, Fulford YO19 4TA — south side of York, conveniently on the way home, free parking
  • ⏰ Sunday trading: the big stores close at 5pm — aim to arrive by ~3:30 to make it worthwhile
  • 🛝 There's an outdoor play area to burn off steam — tag-team the shopping!

6:00pm — Kids in PJs, hit the road at bedtime and let them sleep the ~3¼ hrs home (LU7 3JN). 🚗💤

Tip: Do a final caravan sweep for teddies & Tonies before check-out — and keep the PJs, toothbrushes and pillows out of the boot for the 6pm switcheroo.

🎮 Toddler-friendly games
I Spy (colours) — for the 3 yr old, use colours not letters: "I spy something… green!" 🟢
Spot it! — first to see a tractor / red car / cow / bridge wins a point 🚜
Animal sounds — name an animal, everyone makes the noise 🐮
Sticker books & magnetic toys — mess-free travel activities 📒
Audiobooks & sing-alongs — Julia Donaldson stories are car gold 🎵
Snack countdown — "a treat at the next services!" works wonders 🍪
Guess the animal — give 3 clues: "I'm big, I'm grey, I have a long trunk…" 🐘
Copy the clap — clap a little rhythm, the kids echo it back. Get sillier each round 👏
Colour hunt — pick a colour, first to spot 5 things that colour out the window 🔴
Loud lion, quiet mouse — sing a verse ROARING loud, then whisper-quiet 🦁🐭
Magic word — pick a silly word ("sausages!") and everyone shouts it when you pass a caravan 🌭
Teddy's questions — a teddy "whispers" silly questions to the parent to ask the kids: "have you ever seen a purple cow?" 🧸
